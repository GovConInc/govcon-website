About GovCon.info

We Help Small Businesses Win Government Contracts

More than consultants—we're your partners in navigating the complex government marketplace. Our mission is simple: level the playing field so small businesses can compete and win.

Our Mission

Every year, the federal government spends over $700 billion on contracts. Add state and local spending, and you're looking at trillions of dollars in opportunities. Yet most small businesses never see a dime of it.

Why? Because the system is complicated. The registration process is confusing. The certifications are overwhelming. Finding opportunities takes hours. Writing proposals takes expertise most small businesses don't have.

We exist to change that. We take the complexity out of government contracting so you can focus on what you do best—running your business and delivering excellent work.

The government needs what small businesses offer. We bridge the gap between opportunity and capability.

Our Story

Built on Experience, Driven by Results

Start

Born from Frustration

Our founder started as a small business owner trying to break into government contracting. The experience was frustrating—confusing systems, missed opportunities, and expensive consultants who promised much but delivered little. There had to be a better way.

Build

Learning the System

After years of trial and error, we cracked the code. We learned which registrations matter, which certifications provide real advantage, and how to find opportunities before everyone else. More importantly, we learned how to win proposals.

Grow

Sharing the Knowledge

Other small business owners started asking how we did it. What began as informal advice became a structured consulting practice. We developed systems, built tools, and assembled a team of specialists who live and breathe government contracting.

Today

Your Partner in Growth

Today, we serve clients nationwide across every industry imaginable—from construction to IT, medical supplies to professional services. Our approach remains the same: practical guidance, proven systems, and relentless focus on getting you to contract awards.
