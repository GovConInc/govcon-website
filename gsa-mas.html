FAQ

How long does a typical MAS submission take?

For a prepared company, the build-to-submission window is often 4–6 weeks. Government Q&A and negotiation can extend timelines.

Do you manage the contract after award?

Yes. We offer annual contract management to cover reporting cadence, IFF closeouts, mass mods, and modifications.

What if our pricing model is non-standard?

That’s normal. We structure the offer so the Government can buy it cleanly while staying aligned to your actual commercial practices.
