What it does

Aggregate

Federal + state + local + higher-ed + agency portals—in one index.

Normalize

Standard fields: due date, NAICS/PSC, buyer, location, attachments, and value.

Dedupe

One opportunity, one record—no more wasting hours on duplicates.

Match

Scoring against your NAICS, keywords, past performance, set-asides, and geography.

Alert

Daily/weekly digests and “high-fit” alerts so your team acts early.

Execute

Bid/no-bid workflow, capture notes, and proposal templating.
